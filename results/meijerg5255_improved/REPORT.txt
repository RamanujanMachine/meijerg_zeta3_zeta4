Result
------
With the initial conditions and analytic growth/error rates accepted by the
user, the improved uniform arithmetic argument gives:
  B=0, A!=0:                liminf delta_N > 0.22626234
  B!=0, fixed integer A,B:  liminf delta_N > 0.01634832
Fixed rational coefficients follow by clearing a fixed denominator.
Previous bounds: 0.2190118 and 0.0113626, respectively.
The reduced denominator is used throughout. No irrationality-measure upper
bound or optimal-gcd claim is made.

The new factor
--------------
For p>29, p^2>30n, and fractional part of n/p in
  (1/10,2/19) or (11/12,15/16),
the rational-kernel trace proves
  vp(Q_n)>=3, vp(P3_n)>=0, vp(P4_n)>=-1.
These supply one further common prime factor beyond the first-order shells
in BOTH the pure3 and joint normalizations. The argument deforms each root
k+p*l to k+P*l, applies the residue theorem to F_0 and its P derivative,
then uses the finite-field sum of a polynomial derivative. The cancellation
is uniform for every integer lift of both fractional intervals.

The common divisor now has logarithmic density greater than
  30.45854724380549.
The two new periodic contributions are approximately
  0.50751800694447 and 0.03832125404955.
The shifted cutoff eta=p*ceil(2n/p) depends on the prime. The pure3 and joint
rows use different harmonic orders and lcm powers. Conservation supplies
the common rational readout to which these primewise choices apply.
The finite quarter-prefix matrix cut is not the proof of this improvement.

6. Numerical uncertainties and scope
------------------------------------
Finite deltas and denominator slopes oscillate with prime distribution;
they need not exceed an asymptotic lower bound at every finite N.
No exact limiting delta or optimal gcd is asserted. The uniform arithmetic
conclusion follows from the written trace proof, with symbolic certificates;
finite-depth experiments supplement that proof. Analytic growth and error
rates remain the user's accepted inputs. Exact-error-zero cases are treated
as delta=+infinity; no separate qualitative nonvanishing claim is added.
