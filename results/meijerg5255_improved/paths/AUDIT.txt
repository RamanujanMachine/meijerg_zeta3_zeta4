Conclusion: the trace argument and the affine all-lift certificate are sound.
The two fractional windows are (1/10,2/19) and (11/12,15/16).
No finite-depth extrapolation is required for the arithmetic implication.

For h=-1 residue classes the local high-root kernel is independent of the
residue. Every h=0 kernel differs from it by one monic affine factor. The
formal root deformation has degree -2, so the residue sum and its first
parameter derivative give two exact identities. The polynomial
(X^p-X)F_0 has degree p-2; its derivative supplies the remaining finite-field
sum. Together they make the full trace sum_k p F_n(k+pV) vanish modulo p^2.
The first window's inclusive integer boundaries and the complete algebra
are recorded in work/meijerg5255_improved/paths/trace_audit.tex.

All-lift justification: every high-root interval endpoint is an affine
label (m,c), representing mt+c for x=t+f. The signed boundary Counter for
U_k/U reduces to +1 at (m,c) and -1 at (m,c+1), identically in t. Therefore
U_k/U=V-(mt+c) for every t>=0. This is stronger than checking a selection
of t. The minimal tagged intervals are identical within each Farey cell.
Root count h is unchanged by the lift: numerator and denominator counts
each increase by 30t. The certificate checks open residue cells and their
boundaries. Farey-29 cells suffice since root boundary slopes range from
-17 to 12; differences of slopes have absolute value at most 29.

The affine factors are:
first window: V+16t+2 or V+2t+1;
second window: V+17t+16, V-2t-1, or V-10t-9,
with the permitted subset depending on the fractional Farey cell.

For readouts take representatives 1,...,p and eta=p ceil(2n/p). Moving the
zero residue from representative 0 to p does not affect the trace modulo
p^2 because its h is 1. If p>2n, eta=p<10n; otherwise eta<4n. Thus the
harmonic shift lies in [2n,10n], where the crossed roots are triple zeros.
For j=-k-p ell and k in [1,p],
floor((j+eta)/p)=ceil(2n/p)-ell-1, independent of k.
Also j+eta<19n+p<=29n<p^2. With p>29 and p^2>30n the distinct local high
poles remain distinct modulo p, and all harmonic weights used after
extracting grouped Laurent coefficients are p-integral.

Consequently the local Laurent coefficients yield grouped sums
v_p(sum a_j)>=3 and v_p(sum b_j)>=2. They imply
v_p(Q_n)>=3, v_p(P_3,n)>=0, v_p(P_4,n)>=-1
on every periodic lift of either window. This is one additional p factor
in both normalized arithmetic readouts beyond the accepted first-order
shell factor. No new analytic growth or error assertion is involved.
