\paragraph{Explicit asymptotic delta bounds.}
The exact chamber certificate gives the common-divisor density lower bound
\[
 \gamma_{\mathrm{base}}
 \ge\frac{26236664299512325943}{10^{18}}>26.2366.
\]
It sums 188003 positive terms over 270 exact chambers and the first 1000
periods, rounding every term downward. The omitted terms are nonnegative.
The fourteen additional shell bands, including their integer lifts in $n/p$,
contribute at least
\[
 \gamma_{\mathrm{extra}}
 \ge\frac{3674728195806186818}{10^{18}}>3.674728195806.
\]
This is a second downward-rounded sum, of 47000 positive terms. Thus
\[
 \gamma_{\mathrm{total}}
 \ge\frac{29911392495318512761}{10^{18}}>29.91139,
\]
and it is safe to use $\gamma=29.91139$.
Together with the accepted analytic rates, the certified outward-rounded
constants give
\[
\begin{array}{c|cc|c}
 &\sigma&\tau&\liminf_{N\to\infty}\delta_N\\\hline
 B=0, A\ne0
 &92.540146565866&20.267392681589
 &\displaystyle\ge\frac{20267392681589}{92540146565866}>0.2190118\\[3pt]
 B\ne0
 &111.540146565866&1.267392681589
 &\displaystyle\ge\frac{1267392681589}{111540146565866}>0.0113626.
\end{array}
\]
Here $\sigma$ bounds the exponential height of the supplied integer pair,
and $\tau$ bounds the exponential decay of its linear form.
Reducing the pair can only strengthen these delta bounds: if
$(\widetilde P_N,\widetilde Q_N)=g_N(P_N,Q_N)$ with $g_N\ge1$, then
\[
 \delta_N=-1-\frac{\log|\alpha-P_N/Q_N|}{\log Q_N}
 \ge\frac{\tau N+o(N)}{\log Q_N}
 \ge\frac\tau\sigma+o(1).
\]
All fixed rational coefficients are included after multiplication by a fixed
common denominator. This conclusion concerns the asymptotic quality of
these approximants. It does not assert an irrationality-measure upper bound,
which would require additional appropriate nonvanishing and spacing input.
