Independent audit of the native readout, baseline, and exact carry chambers
========================================================================

1. Native generators and an admissible whole-block order

For w=(a0,...,a4;b0,...,b4), the differential polynomial is
prod(theta-a_i+1)-prod(theta-b_j), of degree4 with leading coefficient
L=5-sum(a)+sum(b). Along the trajectory L=3. A regular complete block
path decreases a2,a3,a4 first, increases a0,a1 next, increases b0,b1,
then decreases b3,b4. Its L stays positive, and the a-b factors needed
by inverse generators stay nonzero. The native implementation in
native.py agrees in all16entries, scalar exactly1, with the independently
cached RT N50 matrix (49trajectory steps).

While only a2,a3,a4 have moved, the differential polynomial has a theta^2
factor. Consequently e0 theta=0 and e1 theta=e0. For negative lengths
ell2,ell3,ell4, put S=(-1)^(ell2+ell3+ell4)ell2!ell3!ell4!.
The whole prefix acts as
  e0 -> S e0,
  e1 -> S[e1+(H_ell2+H_ell3+H_ell4)e0].
This is an exact two-dimensional factorial/harmonic lattice identity.

2. Barnes and rational functionals

The derivation in ../arithmetic/readout_and_baseline.tex passes audit.
The rational density R_w is the gamma quotient there and has poles of
order at most2 along the safe path. Polynomial multiplication does not
increase the pole order. The sums of its double and simple coefficients
are invariant under integer translation and vanish on polynomials.
They annihilate the exact translate-difference form of the differential
polynomial and thus transport as native CMF initial rows. Their initial
rows are (1,0,0,0) and (0,-1,0,0). This proves Q=(-1)^n W00 and W10=0.

The choice of when to deform the contour matters for the third-order
periodic kernel. Deform it only after completing all negative-a blocks:
the resulting rational numerator has triple zeros at integers1..10n.
Both periodic kernels then permit shifting from (0,1) to (2n,2n+1)
without residues. Keep the latter contour for every remaining block.
The differential translate strip is regular because H_w has a triple
zero at2n; the last b4 factor restores the one zero lost in R_w.
Moving the contour during negative-b steps instead would cross simple
poles of K3 R_w and would require explicit residue corrections.

The initial period-integral rows and the endpoint partial-fraction
readouts have the stated signs. K3=-K2'/2 supplies an independent check
of the second initial row. The paired15n denominator intervals and
integer-valued derivative identity yield C_n a_j integral and
d16n C_n b_j integral, where C_n=(15n)!^2/(10n)!^3. The claimed
6 C_n d19n^3 normalization for zeta3 and 6 C_n d19n^4 normalization
for both zeta3,zeta4 follow, including the separate B=0 saving.

3. Farey29 carry chambers and periodic tail

Audited file: ../profiles/exact_chambers.py.
All y-breakpoint slopes lie in {-12,-11,-10,-2,-1,0,1,2,16,17}.
Their pairwise crossings in x have reduced denominator at most29.
The domain endpoints are among the same slopes; the constant-x
breakpoints15x,10x and1/19 also have denominator at most29. The
harmonic cutoff y=1-2x is included. Sampling each open y-cell and each
admissible boundary therefore captures every possible floor signature.
The exact harmonic rational lp is constant on these signatures.

For p^2>30n, every factorial and harmonic index here is below p^2;
the high harmonic indices are less than p. Thus an exact rational
lp=0 establishes cancellation of the leading p^-1 term. If lp!=0,
allowing the loss of one p remains a valid lower bound even when its
numerator accidentally vanishes modulo p.

For x>=1, h=l=1 and the double-pole minimum is
min(s,s+1-loss)=s. Hence the harmonic-zero test drops out entirely.
Simple-pole terms reduce to b+1. These floor expressions are periodic
under integer translations of x and y. Every y-domain then has width
at least1, so it covers all fractional y. This proves the periodic
tail; it does not rely on a false assertion that harmonic numbers
themselves are periodic.

Boundary qualification: for an all-n product require p>29 in addition
to p^2>30n and p not dividing n, or check finitely many small(n,p)
cases separately. The latter two exclusions alone still permit Farey
boundary x=n/p for p<=29, e.g.n1,p7. This correction has no density cost.

Independent computations: all5184 eligible prime instances in the
cached precise profiles n100,500,1000 agree exactly with the continuous
chamber function. A further34 interior rational samples and17 samples
translated to period+2 agree. These checks support the audit; the
finite partition argument above provides the uniform justification.

4. Broad shell 8n<p<11n

Audited ../arithmetic/broad_shell.tex. All five residue ranges and their
integer endpoints give c(k)-d(k)>=-1, with equality exactly on the
stated support. There the high-digit numerator roots are (0,0,0)
and denominator roots (-1,-1,-2,-2), independent of k. The factor
X^p-X supplies the sign -Q(k), and deg Q=p-2.

The local congruence pF(k+pV)=-Q(k)U(V) modulo p extracts a_j/p and b_j
as its double and simple coefficients. For off-support residues both
normalized coefficients reduce to zero. The distinct high-digit nodes
are p-adically separated: n1 has no eligible prime and n>=2 gives p>=17.

At the two supported nodes j=p-k,2p-k the scaled harmonic residues
are respectively0 and1. The verified partial fractions of U yield
constants -8-4/2=-10 and -8-4/3=-28/3. Their sum over k vanishes because
deg Q<p-1. Consequently v_p(Q_n)>=1, v_p(P3)>=-1, v_p(P4)>=-2.
Since v_p(C_n)=-1 on (8n,10n) and2 on (10n,11n), the normalized gains
are1 and4, exactly one above the termwise profile0 and3. The resulting
additional logarithmic prime density is3. No gap found in this proof.

5. Fourteen shells and their periodic lifts

Audited ../arithmetic/periodic_shells.tex and all_shell_cells.py/json.
All47accepted Farey cells were independently sampled at two alternative
rational interior x-values and lifts t=0,1,3, totaling5640 y-cells and
boundaries. Root counts satisfy h>=-1, and the ten tagged high-root
interval endpoints have precisely the stated translations. Merging
only identical endpoint labels yields exactly14intervals and exactly
the manuscript's table. The fixed endpoints after subtraction of
epsilon are stronger information than merely equal readout constants;
they do justify every integer lift.

At a denominator high root l in the translated kernel, the harmonic
index is exactly-l, because floor((j+2n)/p)=epsilon-(l+epsilon).
Distinct root separations are at most floor(29n/p)<p, while harmonic
indices are at most floor(19n/p)<p. Thus the local partial-fraction and
harmonic coefficients are integral at p under the cutoff p^2>30n.
The degree p-2 residue polynomial cancels the leading readout at every
lift. The comparison with the baseline c(f)+1 is correct. The t0 width
is195197/55440; the positive translated density series and its exact
finite lower sum are valid. No new gap found.
